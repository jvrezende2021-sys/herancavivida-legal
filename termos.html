1. About this app

Herança Vivida Publicador ("the App") is an internal tool operated by Herança Vivida, a Brazilian brand that turns family stories into books. The App is used only by the Herança Vivida team to schedule and publish videos created by the brand to its own official TikTok account.

2. Who may use it

The App is not offered to the public. Only authorized members of the Herança Vivida team may connect a TikTok account to it, and only the brand's own official account is connected.

3. What the App does

With the account owner's authorization through TikTok Login, the App uploads videos produced by Herança Vivida and publishes them, or saves them as drafts, on the connected account at planned times. The App does not post on behalf of any other TikTok user and does not read, change or delete other content.

4. Content

All videos published through the App are created by or for Herança Vivida. The brand is responsible for making sure its content follows the TikTok Community Guidelines and Terms of Service, including labeling AI-generated content.

5. Revoking access

The account owner can disconnect the App at any time in the TikTok app settings (Security and permissions → Apps and services) or by contacting us.

6. No warranty

The App is provided as is, for internal use. Herança Vivida may change or discontinue it at any time.
