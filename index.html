Herança Vivida Publicador

Internal publishing tool of Herança Vivida

Herança Vivida is a Brazilian brand that turns family stories, told by the people who lived them, into personalized books. Herança Vivida Publicador is the brand's internal tool to schedule and publish its own short videos to its official TikTok account.

How it works

1. The owner of the official Herança Vivida TikTok account authorizes the app once through Login with TikTok.
2. The team prepares finished vertical videos and captions, and approves each post.
3. At the scheduled time, the app uploads the approved video through TikTok's Content Posting API and publishes it to the brand's own account, labeled as AI-generated content.
4. Weekly, the app reads the account's basic stats and its own videos' performance for an internal report.

Who can use it

The app is not offered to the public. Only the Herança Vivida team uses it, and only the brand's own official account is connected. It does not access, post to or collect data from any other TikTok user.
