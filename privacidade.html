Privacy Policy

Herança Vivida Publicador · Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Who we are

Herança Vivida Publicador ("the App") is an internal tool of Herança Vivida, used only by our team to publish the brand's own videos to its official TikTok account. Contact: herancavivida@gmail.com.

2. What data we access

  • Basic profile information of the connected account (open ID, display name and avatar), through the user.info.basic scope, to confirm the right account is connected.
  • The access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok after the account owner authorizes the App.
  • Information returned by TikTok about the videos we upload (for example, publishing status).

We do not access data of any other TikTok user, and we do not collect followers' personal information, messages or contacts.

3. How we use it

Only to upload and publish Herança Vivida's own videos to its own account and to check that each post was published. We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored privately and are accessible only to the Herança Vivida team. They are kept only while the App is connected and are deleted when access is revoked.

5. Your choices

The account owner can revoke the App's access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security and permissions → Apps and services). To ask for deletion of any stored data, email herancavivida@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Política de Privacidade

Herança Vivida Publicador · Atualizada em 2 de novembro de 2026

O Herança Vivida Publicador é uma ferramenta interna, usada só pela equipe da Herança Vivida para publicar os vídeos da própria marca na sua conta oficial do TikTok. Acessamos apenas as informações básicas do perfil conectado e os tokens emitidos pelo TikTok, somente para publicar e confirmar as postagens. Não acessamos dados de outros usuários, não vendemos nem compartilhamos dados e não os usamos para publicidade. O acesso pode ser revogado a qualquer momento nas configurações do TikTok, e qualquer dado guardado é apagado em até 30 dias após pedido para herancavivida@gmail.com.
